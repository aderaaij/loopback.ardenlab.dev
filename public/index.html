Running coach for iPhone · open source

Your training data, on your server.

Loopback reads your runs, sleep and recovery from Apple Health and syncs them to a training server you host yourself. An AI coach of your choosing plans against that data, and the next workout lands on your Apple Watch.

Loopback needs a server you run. The app is a client for the open-source Loopback Server. Without one there is nothing to sign in to, and no hosted version exists.

How it works

One loop, from plan to run and back.

  1. The coach writes a plan

    An AI assistant connected to your server builds a training plan and queues workouts, using your history as context.

  2. The app sends it to your watch

    Structured workouts with warm-up, intervals, cooldown and pace or heart-rate alerts are scheduled on Apple Watch.

  3. You run

    Apple Watch records the workout as usual. Loopback adds nothing to carry and nothing to start.

  4. The run goes home

    Route, splits, heart rate and running dynamics upload to your server in the background, matched to the planned workout.

  5. Recovery fills in the picture

    Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV and the other domains you chose sync daily, so the coach sees more than the run itself.

  6. The plan adapts

    Missed a session? Say why, then move or skip it. The coach adjusts what comes next.

What you share

Five domains. Four of them are optional.

You choose what Loopback reads from Apple Health when you set it up, and you can change it later in Settings. Whatever you share goes to your server and nowhere else. See the privacy policy for the full list.

Training Required
Runs and other workouts, routes, heart rate and effort.
Recovery Optional
Sleep, resting heart rate, HRV, breathing and blood oxygen.
Body Optional
Weight, body composition and VO₂ max.
Daily activity Optional
Steps and energy burned across the day.
Nutrition Optional
Food and drink you log in other apps.

Get started

Two pieces, both open source.

Loopback Server

A FastAPI and PostgreSQL service that runs in Docker on a home server, NAS or VPS. It stores your training, serves a web dashboard and exposes an MCP endpoint for your AI assistant.

loopback-training-server on GitHub

Loopback for iPhone

Requires iOS 26. Today you build it from source with Xcode 26; a TestFlight build is on the way. An Apple Watch is needed for scheduled workouts.
